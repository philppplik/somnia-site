Planned plans

PlanPlanned priceWhat it is meant to cover
Free$0The core editor, used locally and without a time limit. Planned to include a small monthly allowance of AI requests.
Individual$79 one-timePerpetual license for one person. All v1.x updates included. Advanced code intelligence, more deploy targets and the full component libraries.
Pro$12 per month or $120 per yearEverything in Individual plus optional cloud features and Somnia-hosted AI credits.
Team$19 per user per monthPro plus shared libraries, roles and team management.
EnterpriseCustomTeam plus options such as SSO, self-hosting and custom support terms.

Principles behind the plan

  • Perpetual license. If you buy the Individual license, you own that major version.
  • Subscription is optional. The paid subscription is meant only for cloud and hosted AI features, not for the core editor.
  • No lock-in. Your project is plain HTML, CSS and JS on your disk.

Open questions

The exact scope of the free tier, how a canceled Pro plan falls back, and the seat threshold for SSO are not decided. This page will be updated when they are.

Pricing FAQ

How much will Somnia cost?

Nothing is for sale yet. The current plan is a free tier for the core editor, a one-time Individual license at $79, an optional Pro subscription at $12 per month or $120 per year, Team at $19 per user per month, and custom Enterprise pricing. These numbers are a working plan and can change before launch.

Can I buy Somnia today?

No. There is no installer, no license key and no checkout. The Phase 0 prototype is free to try in a browser.

Will there be a free version?

Yes, the plan is a free tier that keeps the core editor usable without a time limit. The exact limits are not final.

Will Somnia be subscription only?

No. The plan is a perpetual one-time license (Individual) with an optional subscription (Pro) for cloud and hosted AI features.
